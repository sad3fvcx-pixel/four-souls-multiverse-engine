A deal the engine refuses to finish, and what it says about it.
Placebo copies an item's ability; Rainbow Tapeworm becomes a copy of an item;
together they copy each other without end. The rules say nothing about
infinite loops, so no rule was invented: the engine names what kept
happening and stops. Recorded as a gap in docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md 11.5.

$ fsme play --seed 543 --players 2  # shipped content only, no "my sets"

StabilityError: game state did not stabilise within 512 steps. Still arriving when it gave up: stack_push(Placebo) ×10, stack_resolve(Rainbow Tapeworm) ×10, stack_push(Rainbow Tapeworm) ×10, stack_resolve(Placebo) ×10

`fsme play` deals on the default RNG model, 2. The test that holds the engine
to this message, tests/test_runtime_loops.py, deals on model 1 without Guppy's
Paw and uses another seed. A seed is an example of the loop, not a definition
of it: when the deal, or the game that follows it, changes, the seed that
reaches it moves too.
