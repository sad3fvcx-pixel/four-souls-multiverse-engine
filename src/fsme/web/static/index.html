Every step

The game's journal, move by move: every command in the order it was accepted, under the turn it was taken in, with every event it caused — including the bookkeeping the account above leaves out. This is the same file fsme replay reads.

A step is filed under whoever submitted it. That is often not the player it is about: a priority window closes when everybody has passed, so somebody else's pass is what lets your attack resolve. The name in gold beside an event is the player that event concerns — the attacker, through every roll of their attack.
